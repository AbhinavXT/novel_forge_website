The premise · 1 min

Built for people who love reading.

Most reading apps assume you read in 10-minute commutes. This one doesn't. It's built for the person who finishes a series in a weekend and starts another the same night. The kind of reader who keeps half a dozen books in active rotation and remembers which paragraph they stopped at three days later.

That means offline by default, because you read in the subway and on flights. Eleven themes, because your eyes change as the day goes on. Eight fonts, because some days a serif feels right and some days it doesn't. Stats you can look at and think "huh, I read more than I thought," instead of stats that get sent to a dashboard somewhere.

The whole thing is free. The source is open. Whatever's on your phone stays there.

The product · 3 min

Designed like the books it carries.

Built and rebuilt over the past year, mostly on weekends, mostly based on what was annoying about other readers.

The reader

A reader that respects the words.

Eight reading fonts including Literata, Lora, Merriweather, and Open Dyslexic. Eleven themes split between three light and eight dark. Adjustable size, line height, margins. Scroll mode for binge sessions, paged mode when you want to slow down.

Long-press a paragraph to bookmark it or pull a highlight. Reading position is saved on every page turn — come back a week later and you'll be on the same line.

  • 11 themes, 8 fonts, paged or scrolled
  • Bookmarks, highlights, annotations
  • Position saved across restarts

The audio

Text-to-speech that sounds good.

Three engine options: your device's system TTS, plus neural voices from Piper and Kokoro that you download in-app when you want better quality. Speed, pitch, volume, sentence gaps, paragraph gaps — all separately adjustable.

The pronunciation dictionary fixes the words your engine keeps mangling — character names, place names, fictional terms. Save it as JSON and share between devices. Generate full M4B audiobooks with chapter markers when you want to take a novel running.

  • System TTS plus Piper and Kokoro neural voices
  • M4B audiobook export with chapter markers
  • Audio focus respects calls and other media

The stats

Your reading, in numbers.

Word counts, chapters, time spent, daily streaks. A 12-week activity heatmap. Personal records for longest session and best day. Week-over-week percentages so you can see whether you're trending up or just remembering selectively.

Everything's computed locally on your phone. There's no analytics service watching what you read. At the end of the week you can share a summary card if you want.

  • Daily, weekly, all-time stats
  • 12-week heatmap and streak tracking
  • Shareable summary cards

More inside · 2 min

What's in there.

Offline

Works without signal.

EPUBs import in seconds and they're yours. Reading state syncs back to the library on every page. Subway, flight, family dinner — same app.

Hardware

Volume key navigation.

Volume up scrolls up, down scrolls down. Off by default. Audio focus is handled properly, so phone calls and podcasts still work normally when you're not in the reader.

TTS dictionary

Pronunciation dictionaries.

The TTS keeps butchering "Aelindra"? Add it to the dictionary once and you're done. Export the whole dictionary as a single JSON file and load it on another device.

Margins

Bookmarks & highlights.

Long-press a paragraph to bookmark it with a note. Select text to highlight in six colors. Search across every bookmark in a novel. Export the lot as JSON for safekeeping.

Library

EPUB import.

Drag any .epub into the app. Cover, chapters, metadata — extracted automatically. Your whole library lives in one place, organized and searchable.

Hands-free

Teleprompter auto-scroll.

Continuous drift down the page, speed adjustable from 20 to 200 pixels per second. Tap anywhere to pause. Auto-advances chapters with a beat at the top of each new one.

Home screen

Continue-reading widget.

A 2×1 widget that shows the cover and chapter you stopped on. Tap and you're back at the saved paragraph. Updates automatically as your reading position moves.

Portability

Backup & restore.

Export everything — library, reading history, bookmarks, highlights, settings — as a single ZIP. Move to a new phone and restore it all in one step.

No catch

Free, and it stays that way.

NovelForge is free on Google Play. There's no paid tier, no subscription, and no ads — now or later. You don't make an account, and nothing you read leaves your phone. The source is open if you'd like to read it.

Questions · honest answers

The honest FAQ.

Is it really free?
Yes. Free to download on Google Play, with no subscription, no ads, and no in-app purchases. The source is open on GitHub if you want to see exactly what it does.
What about updates?
Google Play updates the app automatically in the background, the same as any other app. There's nothing to do on your end.
What books can I read in it?
Anything you have as an EPUB file. Import it and NovelForge pulls out the cover, chapters, and metadata automatically. Everything stays on your device — the app doesn't host, fetch, or sell content. It's a reader for the files you already own.
Will there ever be an iOS version?
Not planned. An iOS version would mean a separate codebase and a different distribution path, and right now the focus is on making the Android app as good as it can be.
Can I support the project?
Bug reports, feature suggestions, and code contributions on GitHub are the most useful thing. After that, telling other readers it exists. There's no donation page on principle. If you're feeling generous, support an author whose work you've enjoyed — they need it more than this project does.
Where is my data stored?
Locally, in the app's private storage on your device. Reading history, bookmarks, highlights, settings — all of it. The app never sends your reading data to a server because there is no NovelForge server. For backups, Settings has an "Export backup" option that produces a single ZIP you can save anywhere. Importing it on a new device restores everything.
